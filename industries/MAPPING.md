# Industry mapping

Generated by `node industries/mapping.mjs` from `ui/src/utils/customerTheme.js`, `industries/*.json` and `industries/mapping/themes.json`. Do not edit by hand.

The active theme picks the industry. The industry then picks the AI-tab prompts and persona names, and, if it has a pack, the MCP server, tools, Apigee products and Agent Showcase agents. Without a pack the MCP tab and Agent Showcase use the generic Customer Service tools.

**15 of 16 industries have a pack.**
